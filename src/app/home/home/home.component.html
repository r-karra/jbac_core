Foundation & Purpose

Life Enlightenment Foundation (LEF)

Established to bring lasting progress, spiritual solidarity, and cooperative assistance to believers across every walk of life.

Our North Star

LEF Vision

"Serving Christian Community and Unite Christian Community to Serve the AP State."

Dedicated to active community development, health initiatives, higher education scholarships, and purposeful social integration throughout Andhra Pradesh.

flag
Our Mandate

LEF Mission

  • check_circle Inclusive Platform: Bringing all believers together into a single unified platform—from field laborers and domestic workers to civil servants, advocates, doctors, engineers, officers, and students.
  • check_circle Denominational Unity: Uniting the Christian community irrespective of denomination, congregation, or background to serve as one body.
  • check_circle JBAC Council Governance: The Jesus Believers Association Council stewards the execution of all outreach programs, with council membership renewed biennially for fresh vision and leadership.
